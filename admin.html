Before you post

Posting writes a credit line into every open book on that plan, and the same number appears on the public board. Re-posting for a month you've already declared replaces the earlier credits for that month.

The rate you set is a payout obligation. Post a number the shop can actually meet out of trading margin — not one chosen to look attractive.
